    1. Choose a venue. Check for a UK-facing licence, clear rules and payment methods that suit your routine before you register.

    Table Strategies

    • Flat staking: use the same small slice of your balance each roll to smooth out variance.
    • Fixed-target discipline: pick one target that suits your temperament and avoid changing it after losses.
    • Stop rules: decide a win goal and a loss limit in advance, then leave the table once either is reached.
    • Never chase: increasing stakes to recover a loss usually deepens the swing; random outcomes do not correct themselves.
    • Rehearse for free: test any staking plan on practice chips before real money is in play.

    Guest Questions

    1. What exactly counts as a win?

    The roll must land strictly below your target. With a target of 50, the numbers 1 through 49 win and everything from 50 upward loses the stake.

    2. How is the payout multiplier set?

    It reflects the real odds of your target with a small house margin removed. Lower targets mean fewer winning numbers and therefore a larger multiplier.

    3. Is there a best target to choose?

    No setting changes the expected return. High targets smooth the ride with frequent small wins; low targets trade frequency for size. Choose by temperament, not superstition.

    4. Do I need to play with real money?

    No. The practice table uses complimentary chips, mirrors the paid game exactly and is the sensible place to learn before any deposit is considered.

    5. Can a staking system guarantee profit?

    No plan overcomes a negative-edge random game. Staking systems only shape variance; their real value is keeping your bankroll under control.
